Adjectives

Digital

Uses data represented in numbers/digits (in binary).

Virtual

Having an simulated existence within a Computer System, without being a real physical object.

Wireless

Works without any connection cables (Like WI-FI or a bluetooth mouse)

Portable

Can be transported/picked easily (Laptops/Mini PCS).

Programmable

Can be modified with instructions to make specific automatic tasks.

Cybernetic

Related with Social Medias, Robotics and control of Automated Systems.

Electronic

Works with the flow of electricity into the microchips.

Technological

Related to Technology and Science Tools Automated: Works by itself with instructions without constant human's intervention.
